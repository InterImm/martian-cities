Isidis Procyon

A Martian city, explorable.

The 3D city is the InterImm martian-cities SketchUp model. Fly around it, walk its terraces under the Martian sun, then set a population and see what it takes to keep everyone alive.

  • Orbit to look down on the whole settlement.
  • Walk at street level with Mars gravity (3.71 m/s²).
  • Budget shows power, oxygen, water and farmland for any population.

Models come from InterImm/martian-cities. Terrain here is illustrative, not real elevation data yet. Model units are assumed to be metres unless a city says otherwise. Budget figures are rough estimates, not mission data.
